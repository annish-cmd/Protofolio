Our Story

A Decade of Excellence in IT & Security

Founded in 2010 in Kathmandu, Recognized IT Service & Solution began with a simple mission: to provide reliable, enterprise-grade CCTV and networking solutions to homes and businesses across Nepal.

Over the years, we've grown from a small local team to a nationwide service provider. Our portfolio now spans major commercial offices, manufacturing plants, educational institutions, and residential complexes. We don't just sell equipment; we design tailored architectures that solve real security and connectivity challenges.

Today, our certified structural cabling experts, network engineers, and security specialists stand ready to support your organization’s growth.
